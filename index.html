The road leads to your door.

Each stage is a new battlefield with one to four roads. The horde walks them; you decide where it stops.

Support

Most questions are about saved progress. If yours is not answered here, write to us.

How is my progress saved?

ANITARA signs you in with Game Center. There is no separate username or password. Your stage, headquarters, towers, Coins, HQ Parts and power-ups are saved to your ANITARA account on our server each time a battle ends.

I reinstalled the game. How do I get my progress back?

Open the game while signed in to the same Game Center account as before. It signs in by itself and restores your campaign. If you see a different account, check which one is signed in under Settings, then Game Center.

The game says Game Center is required

You are not signed in to Game Center on this device. Open Settings, then Game Center, sign in, return to the game and choose Try again.

You can also choose Play practice to play without an account. Practice progress stays on the device and earns no Coins or HQ Parts.

The game says it could not connect

The game could not reach Game Center or our server. Check your connection and choose Try again. A battle you already started keeps running without a connection, and its result is sent when you are back online.

I closed the game in the middle of a battle

The home screen shows Continue battle. The battle opens paused where you left it. A battle in progress stays on the device where it was started.

Can I buy Coins or power-ups with money?

No. Nothing in ANITARA is sold for real money and there are no ads. Coins come from clearing stages for the first time.

How do I delete my account?

On the home screen, tap the strip with your nickname to open Account, then choose Delete my account and confirm twice. Your account and progress are removed from our server and from the device. This cannot be undone.

If you no longer have the game installed, see the contact box on this page.

Privacy policy

Effective 27 November 2026. This policy covers the game ANITARA on iPhone and Mac and this website. The game is published by JLabs Tech.

The short version

  • We store a Game Center player identifier, your Game Center nickname and your game progress, so that your campaign can be saved and restored.
  • We do not ask for or store your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location or photos.
  • The game has no advertising, no analytics or tracking software, and nothing to buy with real money.
  • We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • You can delete your account and its data at any time, in the game or by writing to us.

What we collect, and why

Our server also receives the internet address of each request, as every server does. We use it only for security, such as limiting repeated sign-in attempts, and we do not build a profile from it.

What stays on your device

The game keeps a copy of your progress, the current battlefield and one unfinished battle in its own private storage. The key that keeps you signed in is stored in the system Keychain. Practice mode is stored only on the device and is never sent to us.

Who else handles the data

Apple verifies your Game Center sign-in. Our server and database run on DigitalOcean, which stores the data on our behalf. Nobody else receives it. We would disclose data only if the law required it.

How it is protected

Everything the game sends travels over an encrypted connection. Sign-in is checked on our server against Apple, and the game never sees or stores a password.

How long we keep it

  • While your account exists, we keep the information above.
  • When you delete your account, the link to your Game Center identity and all sign-in sessions are removed at once, and the copy on your device is erased.
  • The remaining game records, which by then carry no identity, are erased within 30 days.
  • We keep a one-way fingerprint of the deleted identity so that the deleted account is not restored by accident. It cannot be turned back into the identifier. If you play again with the same Game Center account, you are asked first and start from stage 1.
  • Emails you send us stay in our mailbox until you ask us to delete them.

Deleting your account and your choices

In the game, open Account from the home screen and choose Delete my account. Without the game, email jbstreaming02@gmail.com with your support reference; we may ask you to confirm the request so that nobody else can delete your account.

You can ask us for a copy of the data held for your account, or to correct it, at the same address. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under your local data protection law, including the right to complain to your data protection authority.
